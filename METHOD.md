# Method scope

The public repository implements a bounded formalization and audit mechanism.
Its executable public checks use only inline synthetic fixtures. No research
dataset or aggregate result table is bundled.

Historical private work separated three non-pooled studies: Study A used 20
development problems but had no natural wrong-answer opportunity; Study B used
40 controlled cases in 15 clusters and included Gold-derived construction;
Study C was a non-independent author/provenance review. Their raw rows, Gold,
review materials, and predictions are excluded. Those studies do not constitute
a completed publication-grade natural LLM experiment or independently validated
Gold, and this public repository does not reproduce them.
