New source information: one licensed textbook parent with four provisional finite counting subquestions and twelve recorded exclusions. AI feasibility checks pass, but references remain unverified by independent humans. No source independence is implied by four subquestions. Public source exposure and similarity to old cardinality controls limit generalization claims. A human-authored exercise template with WebWork parameters is not a fresh manually authored benchmark instance.

The remaining central gap is independent intended-language semantics. Solvers establish agreement on formal objects; they cannot prove that a human problem was formalized correctly. AI annotation cannot supply independent human Gold, and this agent's audit is not external peer review. All work must retain substantial AI-assistance disclosure.

No evidence justifies claiming broad natural-language reliability, zero population FCR, superiority over cheap-rule on naturally generated errors, independent applicant implementation, or formal Held-out performance. The natural evaluation stopped at the Gold gate, so no baseline results were manufactured.
