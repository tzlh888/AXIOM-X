# Known limitations

- Finite integer domains, at most eight variables and a bounded Cartesian search space; no infinite-domain or unrestricted theorem-proving claim.
- The parser fully matches only a fixed controlled-language grammar. Parser correctness is not formally proved. V4 eligibility used that parser and is therefore parser-conditioned.
- The 96-case challenge contains related families and repeated parameters. There are 49 reference structures after variable-name normalization, not 49 independent observations.
- The four model errors occur on controlled inputs from one task family. Cheap-rule rejects the same four; no added model-error detection over it is demonstrated.
- Python and Z3 can agree on the same mistaken formalization. Internal mathematical checks do not constitute independent human semantic Gold.
- No completed independent human Gold or formal Held-out natural-language validation. Four provisional textbook questions share one parent and may have appeared in model training.
- No claim of unrestricted natural-language effectiveness, zero population FCR or general superiority to simple rules. Nominal intervals and paired probabilities do not solve dependence or sampling bias.
- Lower acceptance coverage accompanies abstention, including abstention on correct but unsupported text. Report invalid and UNKNOWN outputs explicitly.
- Historical model hash prefixes are reported identifiers, not authenticated weight attestations. Generation wrappers contain a hardcoded timestamp; raw response timestamps are retained service metadata, not externally verified timestamps.
- Parser range lists are allocated before the later domain-cardinality check. The prototype is not hardened against arbitrary large or adversarial inputs; trusted bounded research inputs are the supported use.
- The public V5 fixture is a marked licensed text-only derivative. It supports re-extraction of recorded statements, not reconstruction of original webpage assets or byte-identical original website snapshots.
- Applicant independence and mastery are unverified. Substantial AI assistance covers engineering, experiments, analysis and writing. No peer-review claim is made.

Private V1/V2 material is outside this release and cannot be treated as publicly reproduced evidence. V3's auxiliary four-error analysis is post-hoc; it is not pooled with V4. Historical pre-fix V3 analyses are retained in `research_v3/analysis_pre_evaluator_fix/` solely as superseded evidence.
