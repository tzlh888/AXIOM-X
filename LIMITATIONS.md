# Limitations

- The publication-grade natural LLM experiment is incomplete and this public
  repository contains zero natural-LLM result rows.
- Independent blind Gold validation and a pristine independently sourced
  held-out evaluation are incomplete.
- Current public evidence is synthetic mechanism and software evidence only.
  It must not be represented as real-model validation.
- Natural false-certification rate is undefined. Source/specification
  equivalence is unestablished.
- Historical controlled Study B had reference leakage, oracle assistance, and
  label endogeneity; controlled perturbations are not natural errors.
- Historical coverage was 17/40, held-out contamination was HIGH_UNRESOLVED,
  and independent human review was zero. Those underlying private materials are
  intentionally absent from this public source repository.
- Model aliases may drift, and local hashes are integrity records rather than
  trusted external timestamps.
